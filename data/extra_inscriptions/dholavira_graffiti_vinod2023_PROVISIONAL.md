# Dholavira pottery inscriptions: provisional shape transcription (NOT yet in sign numbers)

Source: Vinod V. (2022–23 [2025]), "Signs of the Past", *Heritage* 10: 347–362, Fig. 13 (p. 357) and Table 1 (p. 356). Read from phone screenshots supplied by Erin on 29 Oct 2026. The resolution is low, so every reading is tentative. Shapes are described in words. Assigning Wells or ICIT numbers needs a high-resolution figure, which has been requested from the author.

## Table 1: forms by stage (for distribution, not texts)

Most frequent: numerals (2–6 strokes, single or double rows) and V/chevron forms, in every stage. Stage IV and V carry 79% of the marks. Single signs sit on the vessel base. Multi-sign groups sit on the rim or shoulder. Stamped inscriptions made before firing have 3–4 signs; marks scratched after firing have 4–6.
