# Karanpura graffiti and seals: notes from V.N. Prabhakar's talk (harappa.com / YouTube), screenshots by Erin, 30 Oct 2026

Read from phone screenshots of the slides. All values are approximate, read off charts.

**Graffiti counts.** "Graffiti pattern" chart, N = 130 (main) plus an inset of N = 66. The inset is probably the other phase; its label is not readable.
- Commonest category: "graffiti of different types" (code 100), about 35.
- Y / trident sign (code 91): about 14.
- **Hourglass ⋈** (code 234): about 14.
- Diamond lattice (code 370): about 6.
- Square with dots (265): about 6.
- Strokes (2, 3, 4, 5 strokes and slanting strokes): small counts each.
- Single examples of X, asterisk, comb and rectangle forms.

**Where graffiti sit on the vessel** (N = 130):

| Location | Count |
|---|---|
| Interior of rim | about 39 |
| Body | about 35 |
| Shoulder | about 29 |
| Exterior of artefacts | about 14 |
| Top of rim | about 8 |
| Exterior of rim | about 3 |
| Interior of base | about 1 |
| Outer base | about 1 |

The inset (N = 66) peaks on the body. Compare Rakhigarhi (Nath 2014): belly most common, then rim.

**Drawn marks** (KRP numbers):
- Mostly **single marks**: trees and branches (845, 849, 810, 1162, 603, 112, 627), comb or U forms (851, 810), stars (626, 1013, 569, 630, 908, 700, 1009), grids and lattices (1155B, 1153, 807, 795, 789), and a stylized animal (1010).
- **Hourglass ⋈ on KRP 806 is labelled "Sothi-Siswal phase"** (Early Harappan). KRP 1013 and 815 are labelled "Harappan phase". Sherd 815 has a painted "VN"-like sign plus an incised tree.
- Rim slide titled "Graffiti on rim, Harappan script": a square sign with internal divisions and a zigzag ⋀⋀⋀ / VVV sign. Two or three signs at most.
- No multi-sign graffiti of 3+ signs is legible. **This gives nothing usable for P15, which needs multi-sign texts.**

**Seals.**
- A steatite seal from Karanpura with a bull-like animal. Signs: a spoked wheel, a comb (T with tines) and a stroke. "Sign 118" is marked near the animal.
- It is compared with Lothal L-18 and another seal that carry the same wheel + comb pair, and with Chanhu-daro C-29.
- A terracotta seal with a cross and dots, captioned "Seal to authorize a transaction".

**Cross-site note.** The hourglass ⋈ appears as a graffito at Karanpura from the Early Harappan phase, and at Dholavira (Vinod 2023, Fig. 13 panel 9 and Table 1). It is a candidate for a widespread single-sign owner or potter mark.
