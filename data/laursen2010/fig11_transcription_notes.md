# Laursen Fig. 11 transcription: notes on uncertainty

The figure contains 29 inscriptions: seals 1-28 and seal 56. Signs are listed left to right as drawn. The figure is drawn as seen on the impression, so reading order is not addressed here.

Counts: 126 sign slots in total. 31 high, 43 medium, 21 low (coded) and 31 NOMATCH (all NOMATCH entries are marked low).

## Conventions
- **Long single stroke.** The sheets have two cells that look the same: G31 and G600. I used G31 throughout. Swap to G600 if your corpus uses that code.
- **Twin men.** I coded a pair of side-by-side men as one sign, G91. This applies to seals 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 15 and 24. In seals 8, 9 and 11 the men are joined at the hands, which matches G91 well. In seals 15 and 24 they are drawn apart and could instead be G90+G90.
- **Seal 10.** The pair is a plain man (G90) next to a man with a half-circle bow (G125). I split these into two signs, but they may be one twin compound.
- **Lens with a short inner stroke.** G828, G832, G835, G836 and G837 look the same on the sheets. I used G832.
- **Headless men.** Many men in this figure have no head dot, unlike G90 on the sheet. I still coded them as G90.

## Stroke counts
- **G55 (12 strokes in 3 rows of 4):** seals 3, 7, 9 and the second group on seal 10.
- **First group on seal 10:** drawn as 4/3/4 = 11 strokes. It may be G55 with one stroke lost (low).
- **Seal 11:** six long strokes, spaced as 4 + 2 (G35 + G32). It could also be one group of 6, for which the sheets have no long-stroke cell.
- **Staggered short strokes on seals 3 and 25:** groups of three at different heights, coded G3 (low).
- **Seal 22:** two strokes above one = G13.

## Broken inscriptions (grey patches)
- **Signs lost at the left start:** seals 4, 6, 12, 17 and 18.
- **Signs lost at the right end:** seal 12, the end of seal 10, seal 7 (the second man is partly restored) and possibly seal 14 (last man in the grey).
- **Seal 14:** the top edge is broken, cutting off sign 2 (box with bars).
- **Seal 19:** only part of one man survives at the left. The rest is lost.

## Inscriptions to flag
- **Seal 28** is labelled linear-Elamite in the figure. It is not Indus, and all three signs are NOMATCH.
- **Seal 13** (PCA outlier, Failaka) looks pictorial or pseudo-script. Its signs merge into a scene with a long arrow and a fish or bird. Only G817 (oval with V) is a clear Indus-type sign.
- **Seal 27** looks like pseudo-script or iconography. It has a filled deer device and three non-standard signs.
- **Seal 56:** a U-shaped sign stands on each side of an animal device, which is not a sign. The U shape is coded G700 (low), though it may be a jar (G740) without the ear marks.
- **Seals 7, 26 and 21** have single signs with no Wells counterpart: a sun with rays, a hatched umbrella, a man with triangle arms, and three strokes over an arc.

## Weakest identifications
- Seal 1: sign 2 (lens with triangle) and sign 4 (a trident, while G392 has only two prongs).
- Seal 2: sign 2 (grid-bodied figure, compared with G255) and signs 4 and 5.
- Seal 12: signs 1 and 2.
- Seal 14: sign 4.
- Seal 15: sign 2.
- Seal 16: sign 1.
- Seal 21: sign 5 (G521 or G415).
- Seal 22: signs 2, 3 and 5.
