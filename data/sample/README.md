# Open sample corpus

Generated by `python scripts/make_sample.py`. Edit that script, not the CSVs.

- **MD-TIGER-1**: the real Mohenjo-daro "figure between two tigers" seal (public-domain photograph on Wikimedia Commons). Its signs are recorded as `?` until transcribed from the CISI photograph.
- **SYN-0001 to SYN-0300**: synthetic texts from a small Markov model, flagged `synthetic=true`. They are **not** Indus inscriptions and exist only to exercise the app.
- **signs.csv**: a provisional working list with descriptive IDs. Only JAR has a checked cross-reference (Mahadevan 342).

Replace the synthetic rows with real transcriptions from openly licensed or public-domain sources (e.g. Marshall 1931, Vats 1940), citing the source on every row.
