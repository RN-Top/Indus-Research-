## Step 2: the shapes to match against
- X1: vertical strokes with a looped top
- X2: cross-shaped X with bars on each side
- X3: oval loop with a bar through it
- X4: Y-shaped mark with a narrow stem
- X5: comb of vertical strokes with short rungs (like a ladder)
- X6: a cross-shaped X on its own
- X7: oval with an X through it
- X8: fork or Y-shaped mark with a short stem
- X9: block of parallel strokes with a branch at one end
- X10: small fork or trident with two branches
- X11: ring (O) with a short stroke inside
- X12: four rings in a 2×2 grid
- X13: tall stems with side branches
- X14: triangle with an X and a chevron
