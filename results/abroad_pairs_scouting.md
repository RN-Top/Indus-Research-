# Inscriptions found abroad: sign pairs never seen at home (scouting, exploration only)

*23 October 2026. Script: `scripts/abroad_pairs_scouting.py`; output `abroad_pairs_scouting_output.txt`. Not a confirmatory test. Follows the cuneiform-anchors report (`agent_cuneiform_anchors_2026-10-22.md`, part 3).*

**Idea.** Hunter (1932), Parpola (1994) and Laursen (2010) report that most Indus-script seals from the Gulf and Mesopotamia carry sequences not found in the Indus Valley, and suggest the signs were used there to write another language, probably local names. If so, Indus signs had sound or word values that could be reused for foreign words. This measures it.
