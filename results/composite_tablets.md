# The multi-headed animal on tablets: religious or administrative? (scouting, exploration only)

*20 October 2026. Script: `scripts/composite_tablets_scouting.py`. Find spots and periods from the ICIT export (see `context_scouting.md`). Clay/faience tablets at Harappa and Mohenjo-daro (564 tablets). Moulded copies counted once: identical text + image + excavation area = one batch (468 batches). Not a confirmatory test.*

**Groups.** Mult = multi-headed (composite) animal (23 batches). Ritual = worship scenes, pipal tree, anthropomorphic figure, tree-animal-tree (15). Number = text contains a numeral. Other = the rest.

**Predictions.** Religious: Mult tablets cluster in one or a few spots, sit with ritual tablets, belong to one time slice, carry formulaic texts. Administrative: spread across areas, sit with number tablets, long-lived, varied texts.

| Check | Result | Leans |
|---|---|---|
| Clustering: share of Mult batches in their most common area | Harappa 25% vs 24% for random sets (p 0.63); Mohenjo-daro 25% vs 34% | **Spread out**, no shrine cluster |
| Same areas as ritual tablets | Harappa 17% vs 23% for random non-Mult batches; Mohenjo-daro 25% vs 44% | **Not with ritual tablets** |
| Same areas as number tablets | Harappa 100% vs 95% (number tablets are everywhere: uninformative) | — |
| Time (Harappa, definite period labels) | Mult: 5 in 3B, 1 in 3C. All other tablets: 36 in 3B, 101 in 3C. **Fisher p 0.008**. Ritual tablets: 0 in 3B, 5 in 3C-1 | **One time slice**: the composite animal is a Period 3B image that drops out in 3C |
| Texts | 4+ different texts per city (see `emblem_scouting.md`); 39% contain a numeral; one Mult text, **G840 G32 G740**, is also the full text of worship-scene tablets at Harappa (AB) and Mohenjo-daro (VSA) | Mixed; the shared formula is a real link to the scene tablets |

**Reading.** Mixed. The composite tablets were **not** kept in a special place: they are scattered through ordinary areas like any other tablet, which counts against a shrine-centred cult object. But they belong to **one period** (3B) and then disappear, while worship-scene tablets appear in 3C; and they share a three-sign formula with scene tablets at both cities. A symbol (religious or civic) in fashion for a time and used in everyday transactions or devotions fits best; neither "temple object" nor "permanent state seal" fits cleanly.

**Caveats.** Small numbers (23 batches; 15 ritual); the ritual group is defined by image codes; how the export assigns periods is unverified; Mohenjo-daro has no period labels. Prior work to check: Kenoyer & Meadow on the Harappa moulded-tablet sequence by period.
