# The elephant sign G706 and its partner G705 (scouting, descriptive)

*30 Oct 2026. Project corpus (indus_website). Output: `anchor_paradigm_706_output.txt`. Follows `agent_loanword_anchors_2026-10-29.md` (top lead: elephant, G706 + Akkadian pīru), `copper_anchors.md` and `copper_image_test.md` (copper text predicts image, confirmed on holdout).*

**1. On copper tablets, G706 means "the elephant picture", and its near-twin G705 does not.**

| Copper text | Image | Tablets |
|---|---|---|
| G706 G33 G923 G740 (G1) | **Elephant** | 11 |
| G5 G706 | not recorded | 3 |
| **G705** G33 G923 G740 G1 | **Composite animal** | 1 |
| G235 **G705** G33 G845 G407 G321 G407 | Hare | 12 |
| G806 G235 G233 **G705** G33 G585 G407 | Composite or not recorded | 3 |

- `G705 G33 G923 G740 G1` and `G706 G33 G923 G740 G1` form a **minimal pair**. The texts are identical except for G705 versus G706, and the images differ: elephant with G706, composite animal with G705.
- Every one of the 11 copper tablets with a recorded image and G706 shows an elephant. None of the G705 tablets does.
- **Limit:** the G705 side of the minimal pair is a single tablet.

**2. On seals, G705 and G706 fill the same slot and are interchangeable in one formula.**
- G706 is followed by G33 in 29 of 52 uses.
- The signs found before "G33 G520" are **G705 (23) and G706 (14), and almost nothing else** (G33 and G2 once each).
- G706 is often preceded by a fish-family sign with inside marks: G235, G240, G233, G231 (16 of 52).
- **G706 seals do not show elephants.** They show unicorns (W-type 15) and also zebu 3, gaur 3, and 16 with no recorded animal.

**Reading (speculative, stated plainly).**
- G705 and G706 are two members of one **paradigm**: two words that fit the same slot in a fixed phrase (`[marked fish] + X + G33 (+ G520)`).
- The copper tablets show what one of them refers to: **G706 goes with the elephant.**
- On seals, the phrase "G706 G33" names something linked to the elephant, perhaps a title, office, clan or deity, carried by people whose seals show other animals.
- This fits the two-layer result (paper 3): the text does not label the seal's own animal.
- It also fits Parpola's reading of the copper images as deities or names, and Mukhopadhyay 2021's derivation of Akkadian *pīru* "elephant" from Dravidian *pīlu*. Neither is tested here, and no sound value is assigned.

**Testable next steps.**
1. Check whether "G705 G33" and "G706 G33" seals differ in site, period or animal. That would show whether two named groups or offices used the same formula.
2. Look for other copper minimal pairs: texts differing by one sign with different images. That would give a small **picture–sign dictionary** with controls.
3. Run a prior-work check on Parpola 1994 and 2008 for the G705/G706 contrast. This step must come before any claim of novelty.

## Correction after the prior-work check (30 Oct 2026, same day)

Source: `agent_copper_pairs_prior_work_2026-10-30.md`.
- **The project's own crosswalk maps G704, G705 and G706 to a single Parpola/CISI sign, P316.** In Parpola's transcription they are one sign, with G705 aligned in 10 of 10 texts and G706 in 3 of 3.
- So G705 and G706 are most likely **variant drawings of one sign**, not two words.
- **Confound.** Copper tablets come in sets made together, so a drawing variant that stays the same within a set (all 11 elephant tablets) can come from **one engraver or one batch**. It does not have to mean anything.
- **The G705/G706 "minimal pair" is therefore not evidence of a meaning difference.** It is better described as "same text, different image", which is a known copper-tablet phenomenon (Mahadevan; Parpola 2009: 51).
- **What survives:**
  - G705/G706 (= P316) followed by G33 is a fixed seal phrase, usually after an inside-marked fish sign. It appears on unicorn and other seals, never on elephant seals.
  - The fish-variant swaps that keep the image are a small observation (2 pairs). It fits the project's "fish as modifier" reading better than Parpola's "fish names the deity" reading.
- **G423/G424 (gaur/buffalo) and G63/G61 (tiger)** have no confident Parpola mapping. They need the same check, against Parpola 2008's list and the CISI photographs, before any use.
- **Lesson for method.** Any "minimal pair" must first be checked against the crosswalk, to rule out a drawing variant of one sign, and against set membership, to rule out one engraver.
