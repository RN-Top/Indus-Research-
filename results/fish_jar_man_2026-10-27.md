**Found in exploration.** Texts ending in jar + man (`G740 G90`) have a fish sign right before the jar 51% of the time (23 of 45). Texts ending in the jar alone have one 3% of the time (14 of 536).

**Test data.** The locked ICIT holdout plus the held-out-from-export set: 630 distinct complete texts not used in exploration. Neither had been examined for this relation.

Difference +0.20. Fisher exact p = 0.007 (one-sided); permutation p = 0.006. **Verdict: Supported.** The effect is smaller than in exploration (21% vs 51%), as is usual for a pattern found by looking.

**Reading.**
- "Fish (or a slot sign) + jar + man" is a real ending formula of the script. It is not just a habit of the texts where it was spotted.
- The twelve-stroke texts on the Gujarat coast fit it exactly: Lothal `… G55 G220 G740 G90` and Dholavira `G55 G740 G90`.
- On the man-sign ending, the fish expression is the part that names *which* office or affiliation. Under Parpola's reading the fish is a star or deity name, and under the copper-tablet evidence the fish phrases name figures (`copper_anchors.md`).
