# Do numbers before fish signs behave like star names? (scouting, exploration only)

*26 October 2026. Script `scripts/fish_star_scouting.py`, output `fish_star_scouting_output.txt`. Tests a premise of the twelve-stroke "star-months" reading (Toppe 2026f). Not confirmatory.*

**Idea.** Parpola reads the fish as "star" and numeral + fish as fixed star names, such as "six stars" (the Pleiades). If so, the numerals in front of fish signs should be a few fixed values, more concentrated than the numerals in front of ordinary counted signs. G2 right after an opening sign is excluded, as the project's number rule treats it as a marker, not "two".

**Result: fish are not special.** The values before fish signs are as concentrated as those before other counted signs:
- spread (entropy): 1.39 bits for fish vs 1.36 for other signs;
- share of the most common value: 60% vs 62%.

Several non-fish signs have far more fixed numeral partners: G520 takes three long strokes 97% of the time, and G585 takes seven 91%.

**Details.**
- **Plain fish G220:** two long strokes (G32) precede it 34 times out of 57. Parpola reads this pair as *veḷ-mīn*, "white star" (Venus), taking G32 as "white", not "two". But G32 also precedes the jar G740 19 times, so it is a common prefix in general, not one tied to fish.
- **Jar G740:** its second most common numeral partner is **twelve (G55), 7 times**. Twelve's main partners are the fish *before* it and the jar *after* it.
- **Whole texts "numeral + fish":** only 4 in exploration, each different.

**Verdict.** This check gives no support to "number + fish = fixed star name" as a general pattern. It does not disprove Parpola: individual pairs could still be names. But the "star-months" reading of twelve loses one leg of support. The G55 findings themselves are unaffected: the registered test, the fish-before-twelve order, the gaur and the Bahrain seals.
