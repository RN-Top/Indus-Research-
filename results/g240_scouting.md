# The antenna fish G240, and what stands in twelve's place (scouting, exploration only)

*26 October 2026. Script `scripts/g240_scouting.py`, output `g240_scouting_output.txt`. Follow-up to `g55_fish_im77.md`. Not confirmatory.*

## The antenna fish on its own
- **G240** is a fish with two straight lines sprouting from the top (Mahadevan M67). It has 159 uses in distinct exploration texts.
- It is an **early** sign: 20% of uses open the text, and only 11% are the last sign of the middle (shuffled 29%). It often follows a heading plus G2: `G817 G2 G240` (8), `G861 G2 G240` (7), and `G2 G240` overall (29).
- It forms a **fish pair** with the roof fish: `G235 G240` occurs 26 times. In Parpola's system the roof fish is *mai-mīn*, "black star" (Saturn). He gives no reading for the antenna fish in the sources in the repo.
- After G240 come G233 (14), G740 (12), G520 (11), G636 (8) and G55 (6).
- **No gaur link:** 6.2% of G240 seals show the gaur, the same as all seals (6.7%). The gaur goes with twelve, not with the fish.
- G240 numbers are about 1.5 times the plain fish's after a numeral, so the antenna fish is counted less (numeral right before: 26% vs 41% for the plain fish G220).

Across all of G55's frames, the commonest substitutes are G176, G32, G740, G235, G806, G61, G923, G400, G142, G636, G904, G100 and G760. Several of these (G100, G760, G142, G923) form the registered **end-of-middle slot** (H-2026-10-16-a6cd844c, Supported on the holdout and replicated).

**But twelve is only a loose member of that slot.** It is the last sign of the middle 38% of the time (shuffled 23%), against 83–98% for G100, G760, G142 and G923. It sometimes takes their place, but it is not fixed there.

## A formula: something + jar + man
`G740 G90` (jar, then man) is preceded by:
- the plain fish G220 (12 times)
- G100 (9)
- the antenna fish G240 (8)
- G101 (4) and G176 (4)

The coastal twelve texts fit this formula: Dholavira `G55 G740 G90` and Lothal `… G55 G220 G740 G90`. So a recurring ending is "[fish, or a slot sign, or twelve] + jar + man". Twelve can stand where a fish stands, or come before one.

## Reading
"Fish, twelve, jar, man" looks like the end of a formula naming a person by an office or affiliation. In it, twelve either follows a star or fish expression or takes the place of one. The antenna fish heads such expressions, often right after the opening heading. Twelve comes later and carries the gaur link the fish does not.

**Not shown:** what the antenna fish or twelve means. Parpola's star reading is unproven, and he gives no value for G240 in the sources read.
