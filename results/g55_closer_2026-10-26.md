**Reading.** On texts it was not discovered on, the twelve-stroke sign again tends to close a content group rather than count the next thing. So G55 is not just "12" used like the other numerals. It acts more like a word in its own right: a fixed number-word such as "the Twelve", a unit, or a title or name part. That fits the Gulf seals, where it stands by man signs.

**Limits.**
- 11 G55 tokens. The p-value is modest, and a single test with no correction for other tests.
- The secondary result pointed the same way but was not significant.
- The test cannot say *which* non-count meaning G55 has.
