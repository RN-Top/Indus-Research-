## The tags are the Gujarat coast
All five G55 tags come from three sites:

| Site | Tag |
|---|---|
| Lothal | `G705 G500 G741 G1 G55 G220 G740 G90` |
| Lothal | `G55` |
| Dholavira | `G55 G740 G90` |
| Dholavira | `G55 G740` |
| Harappa | `G55 G255 G435 G690` |

Overall: 5 of 52 G55 texts are tags, against 4.2% for other texts (Fisher p 0.07). But tags themselves are concentrated at the ports Lothal (55) and Dholavira (38), so the tag result and the coastal "twelve + jar + man" result are **the same pattern seen twice**, not two independent findings.

**Combined picture:** at the Gujarat ports, goods were tagged with "twelve (+ fish) + jar + man". In Dilmun, the Indus-related merchants put twelve on their seals. **The reading that fits best is a merchants' body or office, "the Twelve", handling goods in the sea trade.** The data can't tell whether the name came from the year.
