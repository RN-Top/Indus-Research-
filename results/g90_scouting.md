**At home G90 is an ending, not a name marker.**

| G90 at home (70 uses) | G90 | 16 signs of similar frequency (mean) |

52 of its 70 uses come right after the jar G740: "jar + human figure" is a closing formula at home.

**Abroad it behaves differently (descriptive, seen after the abroad-pairs test).** In the 6 abroad texts with G90, it is never after the jar and is first or second in 5 of them: Ur `G90 G1`, `G90 G405`, `G350 G90 G4`; Failaka `G90 G817 G317`; Susa `G365 G90 G407 G604 G700`. Only one (Failaka `G716 G350 G1 G90`) has it last.

**Reading.** The same sign does a different job abroad: at home it closes texts after the jar; abroad it opens or sits near the front, as Laursen's "man" name prefix would. This fits the abroad-pairs result (familiar signs, new combinations) and suggests the signs were reused with their values, not their home formulas. Several abroad texts are broken, so positions there are uncertain.
