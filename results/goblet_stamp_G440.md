# The goblet stamp G440: one sign, mass-produced cups, two settlements (scouting, descriptive)

*30 Oct 2026. Merged ICIT corpus (paragorth canonical, which merges some variant drawings; 92% of sign numbers are identical to the project corpus). Builds on `pottery_stamps_and_bone_scouting.md` and `agent_function_anchors_2026-10-22.md` 2a.*

**Facts.**
- G440 is **stamped alone** before firing on 12 Harappa pots: H-1756 to H-1762, H-1092 to H-1098, and one more. Every dated one is **Period 3C**, and the stamps are square.
- These are Kenoyer's stamped **pointed-base goblets**: mass-produced, single-use cups.
- **The same stamp is on six goblet sherds at Lahoma Lal Tibba, about 24 km from Harappa.** Function-anchor report: "Six sherds at Lahoma Lal Tibba carry the same stamps as H-1756 to H-1759". So a G440-stamped batch of cups reached a second settlement.
- The other goblet stamps from 3C are also short:
  - G416 alone (rectangular)
  - G13 alone, a stacked-stroke numeral
  - G540 G540
  - G33 G220, three long strokes + fish
- **On seals, G440 is an ordinary script sign.** It occurs on 32 seals, 11 tablets, a bangle and a rod.
  - Position: mostly in the middle (30), first 14, alone 12, last only 3.
  - Often just **before the closers** G740 or G760 (right neighbour G740 12×, G760 6×).
  - Often just **after the fish** G220 (9×). A typical shape is `... G220 G440 G760 G740`.
- G440 seals show the unicorn less often than seals in general: 16 of 26 identified, against 75% overall (p ≈ 0.09, one-sided). They include tiger 3, elephant 3 and gaur 3. Not significant.

**Reading.**
- A single script sign was used as the **production or distribution stamp** on a mass-produced vessel type. The stamped cups travelled at least 24 km.
- The same sign appears inside seal texts in the slot **just before the closing formula**. That is the slot where the project's confirmed "final-middle" signs sit (`H-2026-10-16-a6cd844c`).
- One reading that fits: G440 names an **institution, office or producer**. Stamped alone, it marks goods made or issued under that office. Inside a seal text, it gives the holder's affiliation before the closing formula.
- This is a ground in the archaeological sense: a sign tied to a known object type, a known date (3C), a known practice (stamping before firing) and a known distribution (Harappa → Lahoma Lal Tibba). It is not a reading.
