## Results

- Counts are descriptive only. No agreement rate, sign comparison or significance test was run.
- ICIT has no field for upper or lower levels. The "upper levels" restriction on the plate could not be applied. The `period` and `phase` fields are mostly blank or '-' (673 and 677 of 1,341), so they can't serve as a level filter either.
- Falsifier check: ICIT has DK and SD seal objects, so the falsifier did not trigger. The area mapping can be attempted in principle.

## What this does and does not show

- The plate and ICIT both use the same Mohenjo-daro DK and SD areas, so a match by area is possible.
- The DKH and SDStupa matches are small (7 and 4 objects). Matching 18 plate seals to ICIT objects by area alone would give many candidates per seal, so the match cannot be unambiguous. P18's requirement of an unambiguous match cannot be met by area alone.
- Area-only matching cannot test ICIT's transcription accuracy. This count alone supports nothing about ICIT's accuracy.
