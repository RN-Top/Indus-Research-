71 sealings carry 61 distinct seal texts:

| **The twelve seal** | **11** | **5** | **7** (straw, box, sack, pot, structures, door, unclear) |

| Any other seal | ≤ 3 | ≤ 2 | ≤ 2 |

5 of the twelve seal's 11 sealings are co-sealed, against 7 of the other 60 sealings (Fisher p ≈ 0.016, one-sided, after looking).

## Reading
At Lothal's warehouse the holder of the twelve seal:
- sealed the most closures;
- sealed the widest range of things (straw packages, boxes, sacks, pots, cages, storeroom structures, a door);
- **is the only seal that regularly countersigned with other officials**, five different ones, one of whom held a seal ending in a man sign.

The box seal, by contrast, worked alone on one kind of container. That is the profile of a supervising office, the person whose seal accompanies others' when storerooms are opened. It fits Frenez's "shared administrative responsibility" and the "title, office" reading of twelve (`g55_group_models.md`). Paragorth S226 recorded the twelve seal's closure range. The co-sealing role and the 11-sealing count are new here.
