# Does a Lothal seal stick to one kind of closure? (scouting, descriptive)

*29 October 2026. Script `scripts/lothal_text_closure.py`, output `lothal_text_closure_output.txt`.*

**Data.**
- Seal texts per sealing: paragorth's merged ICIT corpus.
- Closure types: Frenez & Tosi 2005, Table 1.
- 55 of the 71 Lothal sealings with texts have a diagnostic closure type.
- The data had already been partly examined (`lothal_closures.md`, `lothal_cosealing.md`), and it includes sealings whose texts fall in the holdout split. No frozen test uses closure type. **Not confirmatory.**

**Statistic.** Among pairs of sealings stamped by the same seal, what share closed the same kind of thing? This is compared with 10,000 shuffles of closure types among the sealings.

| | Sealings | Same-seal pairs | Same closure | Chance | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All seals | 55 | 84 | **0.61** | 0.16 | 0.0001 |
| Without the box seal (`G861 G2 G48 G740`) | 45 | 39 | **0.15** | 0.17 | 0.58 |

**Reading.** One seal, the elephant box seal, sealed only wooden boxes. Every other reused seal, the twelve seal included, sealed **whatever needed sealing**, at chance rates. So at Lothal a seal text does not name a commodity or a kind of container. It goes with the person or office doing the sealing. This matches Frenez (2020: 27, "no clear patterns ... sharing of ... administrative duties"), Mukhopadhyay (2023, texts as issuing authority, not goods), and the co-sealing result. The box seal is the exception. It could be one consignment, or an office in charge of boxes.

**Limits.**
- One site, with only 4 reused seals once the box seal is excluded.
- Closure types are as Frenez & Tosi classified them.
- Some second impressions are unread. Parpola 2007 would help.
