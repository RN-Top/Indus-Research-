# Moulded tablets and the jar sign (scouting, descriptive)

*27 October 2026. Scripts `scripts/moulded_tablets.py` and `scripts/jar_sign.py`; outputs in `results/`.*

## 3. Moulded tablets (mass-produced tokens)
- 500 moulded tablets (ICIT code TAB:B), almost all from Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, carrying 317 distinct texts. 30 texts were made in 3+ copies.
- **Only 30 of the 317 moulded texts also occur on seals.** The tablets have their own vocabulary: signs such as G501, G711, G318, G347 and G436 are 5–6 times commoner on them than overall.
- **Most-copied texts:**
  - `G501 G405 G2 G240 G520` (29 copies)
  - an 11-sign text (11 copies)
  - `G630 G740` (10)
  - **two texts ending in jar + man:** `G347 G741 G176 G740 G90` (8) and `G104 G645 G590 G235 G240 G740 G90` (7). The second has the antenna fish G240 right before the jar.
- **Twelve on moulded tablets:** four, all with the unit `G255 G435`: `G55 G255 G435` (H-703), `G55 G255 G435 G690 G921 G400` (H-209), `G740 G690 G435 G255 G55` (H-1797, the same phrase in reverse order), and Mohenjo-daro M-488. The same phrase opens H-2584, the multi-seal administrative sealing (`g55_lothal_sealings.md`). **"Twelve + G255 G435" is a fixed phrase used on tokens, on a record sealing and on seals.**

## 4. The jar sign G740
- 783 uses in exploration: last sign 69%, followed by the man sign G90 in 52 cases.
- Signs before the final jar: the end-of-middle slot signs (G760, G100, G390, G176, G923).
- **Fish before the jar when the man follows:** 51% in exploration, confirmed on unseen texts (21% vs 1.6%, p 0.007; `fish_jar_man_2026-10-27.md`).
- **Numerals right before the jar:** 51 of 783. Two long strokes G32 (22) and **twelve G55 (8)** lead.
- **Reading:** the jar closes a descriptive phrase. When a man sign follows, the phrase before it is usually a fish expression or twelve. That is the shape "[affiliation] + jar + man", consistent with the jar as a title-forming or genitive-like ending ("he of …") and the man sign as the person. The script cannot yet say which.
