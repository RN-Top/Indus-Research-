**Bottom line.** Published material beyond ICIT adds roughly **5–30 texts**, most of them short or broken. That is too few for a test on its own, and below the 30 seals the sealed prediction P8 needs. The realistic routes to more:
1. Ask Fuls for objects added to ICIT after the 2022 snapshot.
2. Ask the Bagasra/Shikarpur team (Ajithprasad, Jamison) and the ASI (Binjor, Lothal, Rakhigarhi) for seal photographs.
3. Ask Juan what he holds.
