# Do written numbers follow the Indus weight series? (scouting, exploration only; not registered)

*21 October 2026. Counts from `number_system_scouting.md` (exploration split; values are stroke counts read from the sign drawings).*

Indus cubical weights follow a binary-then-decimal series (ratios 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, then 160, 200, 320, 640, …). If written numerals recorded weight units, the values in reach of the script (1–12) should favour the series members 1, 2, 4, 8 over their neighbours.

| Value | 1 | 2 | 3 | **4** | 5 | 6 | 7 | **8** | 9 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short strokes | 112 | 173 | 79 | **43** | 22 | 2 | 3 | – | – | – |
| Long strokes | 100 | 172 | 123 | **11** | 10 | – | – | – | – | – |
| Stacked strokes | – | – | 14 | **2** | 4 | 20 | 38 | **6** | 3 | 33 |

- 4 is not enriched: short strokes 43 against a geometric mean of its neighbours of 42 (3 and 5); long strokes 11, below 3 (123).
- Stacked strokes peak at **7** (38) and **12** (33), neither a weight value; 8 has 6.

**Verdict: no sign of the weight series.** Written numbers behave as small counts (falling off from 2) plus fixed formula numbers (7, 12), not as weight units. Not registered for the holdout because the exploration split already contradicts it.
