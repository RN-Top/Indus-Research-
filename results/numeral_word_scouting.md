# Which numerals act like counts and which like words? (scouting, exploration only)

*26 October 2026. Script `scripts/numeral_word_scouting.py`, output `numeral_word_scouting_output.txt`. Follow-up to `g55_closer_2026-10-26.md`. Not a confirmatory test.*

For every numeral with 10+ uses: the share of uses where the next sign is a content sign (the counted thing) and where it is a closer.

- **Count-like:** the stacked and short strokes 3–7 (G3, G4, G5, G13, G16, G17) come before a content sign 57–79% of the time, and before a closer 0–19%.
- **Parts of bigger numbers:** the small strokes 1 and 2 (G1, G2, G31) are often followed by another numeral (22–24%), as in compound numbers.
- **Word-like:**
  - **G55 (12):** content 30%, closer 36%. This was confirmed on held-out texts.
  - **G35 (long 5):** content 20%, closer 30%, but only 10 uses.
  - **G33 (long 3):** closer 33%. This comes almost entirely from the fixed pair **G33 G520** (31 times). It is a known unit, not a general habit of the sign.

**Reading.** G55 is the one clear number-word. G35 is a candidate that is too rare to test. G33's high closer share comes from one formula, G33 G520, which agrees with the earlier finding that "3 + G520" behaves as a fixed unit, though the broader counted-vs-formula idea failed.
