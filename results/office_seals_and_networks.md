# Office seals, what they sealed, and the same seal in two cities (scouting, descriptive)

*27 October 2026. Scripts `scripts/office_seals_scouting.py` and `scripts/same_seal_sites.py`; outputs `office_seals_scouting_output.txt` and `same_seal_sites_output.txt`. Lothal closures from Frenez & Tosi (2005), "The Lothal sealings", Studi Egei e Vicinorientali 1, Table 1, as transcribed in paragorth `derived/lothal-sealings-frenez-tosi2005.json`. All data already seen; descriptive.*

## 1. Seals used again and again (office seals)
182 sealings and tags carry 153 distinct seal texts. Only 10 texts were stamped twice or more at one site, and three dominate:

| Uses | Site | Seal text | What it closed (Frenez & Tosi 2005) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Lothal | `G817 G2 G48 G740` | wooden boxes only (L-161 to L-170), all in the warehouse |
| 8 | Kalibangan | `G91 G56 G2 G417 G890 G892` (**starts with the twin-man sign G91**) | not recorded |
| 7 | Lothal | `G705 G500 G741 G1 G55 G220 G740 G90` (**twelve + fish + jar + man**) | **five kinds**: woven straw (L-124), wooden box (L-125), leather sack (L-126), pottery (L-129), **door on a peg** (L-206, with a second seal); L-127 and L-128 unclear. **All in the warehouse.** |

**Reading.**
- The box seal is a specialist: one kind of container.
- The twelve seal is a **generalist with authority over the whole storeroom**: doors, boxes, sacks, jars and straw packages.
- Two of the three busiest seals in the corpus carry a man sign, and the Kalibangan one has a stroke-block sign (G56) right after its twin-man sign. G56's exact form is not in the project's sign files; it sits in Wells's stroke-block range (G55–G59), so whether it is a twelve-like block needs checking against the sign list (Fuls 2023, p. 22).

## 2. The same seal text in two cities
31 texts of 3+ signs occur at two or more sites. In three of them, the text is a **seal at one site and sealings at another**:

| Text | Seal found at | Sealings at |
|---|---|---|
| `G817 G2 G48 G740` (the Lothal box seal) | **Mohenjo-daro** (1 seal) | **Lothal** (10 sealings), about 900 km away |
| `G705 G33 G520` | Mohenjo-daro (2 seals) | Lothal (1) |
| `G861 G2 G3 G390` | Mohenjo-daro (2 seals) | Kalibangan (1) |

Either seals or sealed goods travelled between the cities, or the same office used identical seals in several places. Both readings mean **offices that operated across the civilization**, fitting Frenez's "shared administrative responsibility" and the one-script, one-layout result (preprint 3 v1.3).

The twelve seal's text occurs nowhere else: it was a Lothal office.

Many other shared texts are moulded tablets at Harappa repeating seal texts from Mohenjo-daro (for example `G176 G740 G400`, 26 tablets). That is the subject of (3).
