# Seal size and headings (EXPLORATION)

*20 October 2026. Exploration split only. Script: `scripts/seal_size_heading.py`.*

**Data:** 970 seals with recorded face size.

**Raw pattern:**
- Small seals have a heading (an opening sign) 17% of the time.
- Larger seals have one 30–33% of the time.

**Controlling for text length.** Bigger seals carry longer texts, so the comparison was made within each text length.
- The size–heading correlation is **0.063**, p = 0.03, one-sided, with labels permuted within each length.
- The effect comes almost entirely from **4-sign texts**: 22% have a heading on smaller seals, 42% on larger ones.
- Lengths 3, 6 and 7 show nothing, or a slight reversal.

**Verdict:**
- **Weak.** Most of the raw size difference is explained by text length.
- Not worth a locked test on its own.
- Kenoyer's "seal size reflects rank" is **not clearly visible in the headings.**
