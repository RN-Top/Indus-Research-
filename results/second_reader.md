# Second reader for the enclosure-mark annotation

*21 October 2026. Sheet: `data/sign_marks/second_reader_sheet_2026-10-21.png` (30 variant/base pairs drawn in the SK Indus font, numbered in a shuffled order); key: `second_reader_key_2026-10-21.json` (committed only after the answers were in). Reader: Erin Rae Toppe, blind to the key and to sign positions. Question: "does the left sign have short strokes at the corners/sides of the right sign? Yes / No / Unsure." The 17 pairs tagged `enclosure_strokes` in `marks_blind_2026-10-20.csv` were mixed with 13 other variant pairs.*

| Pairs | Yes | Unsure | No |
|---|---|---|---|
| The 17 enclosure pairs | **11** | 6 | **0** |
| 7 pairs whose added mark is inside or on top (dot, inner line, crossbar, antennae, roof) | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| 6 pairs whose added marks are side strokes or brackets | 6 | 0 | 0 |

- **No enclosure pair was rejected.** The six "Unsure" are mostly fish variants that also carry an inner line or crossbar (G232, G234, G241, G226), plus G680 and G807; the reader judged the extra inner mark made the match uncertain.
- The six side-stroke and bracket pairs were all "Yes". The question said "corners/sides", so these are correct readings of the question, not errors: **the sheet did not separate four-corner strokes from side strokes and brackets.** Both readers agree that all of these are outer marks.
- Inner and top marks were mostly rejected or doubted (5 of 7).
- The reader suggested testing inner marks, and inner + outer together, as their own classes.

**Consequence.** The second reading supports the annotation of the 17 pairs as carrying outer strokes (11 yes, 0 no). It also shows the boundary between "enclosure strokes", "side strokes" and "brackets" is not obvious to a fresh eye; a broader "outer marks" class is a natural next scouting test.

**Process note.** The reading was done by voice with Claude reading out the instructions; the key was not available in that conversation, and Claude did not say which pairs were enclosure pairs. One reader, one session.
