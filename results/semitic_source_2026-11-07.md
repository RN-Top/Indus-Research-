# Licensed Semitic transcription source (7 Nov 2026, local, not committed)

**Candidate:** Brown University, Inscriptions of Israel/Palestine (IIP), github.com/Brown-University-Library/iip-texts, folder `epidoc-files/`. EpiDoc XML with diplomatic and edited transcriptions, and English translations.

- Counted in the clone: 1,697 files tagged `xml:lang="arc"` (Aramaic) and 379 tagged `he` (Hebrew). Language tags were not checked against the text itself.
- Transcription text was not yet extracted, and no units were built.
