# Sign key routes, checked 7 Nov 2026 (local, not committed)

## 1. Unicode proposal L2/99-159 (Everson, 1999)
- Gives a numbered sign list, H001–H386, with names and order said to match Parpola 1994 and 1996.
- No copyright or licence statement in the document.
- Glyph shapes did not come through in the text extract, so the list cannot be matched to plate shapes from it.
- Status: names only. Not a licensed shape key. Unicode terms of use not yet confirmed (the Unicode copyright page is not on the allowlist).

## 2. Roboflow "indus_valley_script" dataset
- CC BY 4.0. 95 images, classes "Animal", "objects", "symbol", for instance segmentation.
- No description; images appear to be seals and tablets (filenames only).
- Status: not a sign key. The images themselves were not reachable from this session.

## 3. Zenodo 18421163 (Dominik, "Indus Transaction Grammar", CC BY 4.0)
- A seven-page grammar paper. The record says it has no corpus cases and no sign list.
- Status: excluded. Nothing usable for a sign key.

## Route B (shape key from plate LXXXII)
- Not started in this session. Each distinct shape needs cropping and a provisional X-code under RULES section 3, with the plate and seal locator, then a second reader.

## Hybrid (H-numbers as names)
- Names alone cannot match plate shapes. Without a shape reference, any H-number assigned to a plate shape would be guesswork. Not done.
