# Structure → meaning: what each part of an Indus text is for (synthesis, 1 Nov 2026)

This note pulls together the project's confirmed tests (C), scouting results (S) and outside anchors (A) into one map. It says **what kind of meaning** each slot carries, not what any word sounds like. Every line points to the evidence; nothing new is claimed here except the arrangement.

## The template

A full text, read from right to left and shown here in reading order:

`[HEADING]  [NAME / CATEGORY]  [COUNT or MEASURE]  [AFFILIATION]  [CLOSER]`

| Slot | Typical signs | What it does | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heading** | G861, G820, G817, G920, G692, G503 (+ G2) | A fixed opening formula, like "seal of" or a title. It does not change what follows. | Openers start texts 50–88% of the time (S `functional_slots`). The order transfers across all 7 sites (C, LOSO, p ≈ 2e-18). The opener predicts no other feature (S `heading_types`). |
| **Name / category** | Rare first signs in texts without a heading | Who the text is about. These are categories or titles shared across the civilization, not local personal names. | First sign is rare (C `H-2026-10-16-51b7445b`). Rare signs are not local (S `rare_sign_locality`). Mukhopadhyay 2023 argues there are no proper names (A). |
| **Count** | Short strokes G1–G7 + an item sign (G220 fish, G390, G240, G803, G840) | "N items". Short strokes count things. | The two stroke families take different following signs (C `97716181`). G32 → fish (C). Counted items spread over more values than formula items (C `numeral_spread`). An inside mark stops the fish being counted (C, preprint 4). |
| **Measure** | Long strokes G31–G36 + **G700** | "N units of about 40 L". This is volume, not a count. | G700 goes with long strokes 33% vs short 1%, and in Mahadevan's transcription 29% vs 0/906 (S). Wells's measured pots: 40.4 L per stroke (A, Wells 2015 Table 4.1). Unit tablets: same text, same amount, across find areas (S `ration_chit`). |
| **Affiliation** | Signs just before the closer: G440, G100, G760, G142, G923 | The office, producer or group the text belongs to. | The final-middle set sits before the closer (C `a6cd844c`). G440 is stamped alone on mass-produced goblets and travelled 24 km (A, Kenoyer & Meadow 2010; S `goblet_stamp_G440`). |
| **Closer** | G740 (jar), G520, G400, G90, G151, G390 | The ending formula; it marks the genre (seal vs tablet). | Closers end texts 66–88% of the time (S `functional_slots`). G740 ends texts (C positive control). G400 marks the tablet genre (C `2e6e5589`). Corner or enclosing marks push a sign to the end (C `be9ab5ab`). |

## Layers beyond the line of text

| Layer | Meaning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Image** (animal, trough, standard) | Workshop or issuer emblem, separate from the text | Text does not predict the animal (C `74e127e6`). Unicorn W-type seals are larger (C `d797caf3`). Carving style is regional while writing is uniform (S). The trough belongs to the animal (S). |
| **Marks on signs** | Grammar: top marks move a sign earlier, corner marks move it to the end, inside marks change its partners | C `b2aa3439`, C `be9ab5ab`, C P9 (`b09485c6`) |
| **Who sealed, not what** | A seal text names the official or office, not the goods | At Lothal, seals other than the box seal close any kind of container at chance rates (S `lothal_text_closure`). Frenez 2020: the text is readable and the animal often is not (A). |
| **Writing in daily life** | Owners scratch the same script on pots, in the same direction, with about twice the numerals | S `pottery_vs_seals` (robust part) |

## What this adds up to

Many Indus texts are **administrative entries**: a heading, a named category or office, a count of items **or** a measured quantity of about 40 L units, an affiliation, and a closing formula. Rations and allotments (the unit tablets), goods marked by an issuing office (the goblet stamp), and officials sealing storerooms (Lothal) are three independent archaeological contexts that fit this reading.

**Not established:**
- sound values;
- which commodity G700 measures;
- what any individual name or category sign means.

## Example of the measure slot (unit tablets, Harappa)

- `G413 G575 G335 G717 G33 G700` appears on 10 moulded tablets: "[...] G717, 3 units" (about 121 L).
- `G413 G575 G335 G740 G32 G700` appears on 2 moulded tablets: "[...] G740, 2 units" (about 81 L).
- The frame is the same, but the item sign differs, and so does the amount.
- G717 (Parpola P320) and G740 (P324) are different signs. G717 is followed by G33 (three long strokes) in 15 of its 77 uses.
- This is a candidate **item-in-the-measure-slot** contrast (n = 2 moulds; scouting).
