## Update, 7 Nov 2026

- **Aramaic:** Erin accepted the Elephantine CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence for research reuse, with attribution, on 7 Nov 2026. Registry row set to "checked". The object page has no transcribed text, so no units are available yet.
- **Prakrit, candidate 1:** Farris & Arora, "For the Purpose of Curry" (arXiv 2111.12783), an Ashokan Prakrit treebank of 12 documents (973 tokens). No dataset location or licence is given, so it is not usable yet.
- **Prakrit, candidate 2:** Bhavnagar Archaeological Dept, "A collection of Prakrit and Sanskrit inscriptions" (c.1894), on SouthAsiaCommons. The rights statement reads "Public domain only when viewed in the US" (HathiTrust). That is not an open licence for research reuse, and the transcriptions are not confirmed. Not accepted.
- **Indus (plate LXXXII):** Looked at the plate. Reading the signs needs G-number mapping from `data/glyph_map.csv`, and that has not been done. Plate LXXXII has 18 seals, too few for the battery's statistics on their own.

## Prakrit search, continued (7 Nov 2026)

- **aso2101/prakrit_texts (GitHub):** TEI XML, CC BY-SA 4.0 per the README (no LICENSE file seen). Content is literary and religious Prakrit (Gaüḍavaho, Gāthāsattasaī, Āyāraṅga), not inscriptions. Genre mismatch for an inscription control. Not used.
- **SIDDHAM (siddham.network):** "open-access reference tool" covering Prakrit inscriptions. No licence or reuse terms stated; no statement on downloads. Not accepted.
- **Hultzsch, Inscriptions of Asoka (archive.org):** full text is online. The licence and its public-domain basis are not recorded. Not yet checked under amendment item 2.
- **Zenodo 14795104 and 18206503:** Ukrainian and Armenian epigraphic corpora, not Prakrit. Not used.
